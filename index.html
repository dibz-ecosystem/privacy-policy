Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the individual developer responsible for DIBZ ("DIBZ", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the DIBZ mobile application ("App"). It describes the information practices for the App and how you can contact us about privacy.

Before publishing: This policy is a starting draft. Remove services or data types that do not apply, and add any actual collection or sharing that is missing. Do not submit an inaccurate policy.

1. Information we may handle

Depending on the features you use and the information you provide, DIBZ may handle:

  • Account information: information you submit when registering or managing an account, such as your name, email address, phone number and account credentials.
  • Profile and user content: profile details, profile images, preferences, messages, reviews or other content you choose to submit, if those features are available in the App.
  • Order and transaction information: details needed to create, fulfil or support orders and transactions, such as items, order identifiers, amounts, transaction status and related customer or vendor details, where applicable.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting us for help.
  • Technical information: information that may be generated when the App is used, such as device type, operating system version, app version, diagnostic information and log data, where collected by the App or its service providers.

Do not send us passwords or one-time verification codes through support email. We do not ask you to disclose them.

2. How we use information

We use information only as needed to operate and maintain DIBZ, create and secure accounts, provide the features you request, process and support orders or transactions where applicable, communicate service-related information, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, troubleshoot problems, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared when necessary with service providers that help operate the App (for example, hosting, database, communications, payment or infrastructure providers), with other users where a feature requires it (for example, relevant order information shared between a customer and vendor), when you ask us to share it, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety and security.

The developer must list the actual third-party providers used by the production App and describe what information each receives. This may include authentication, cloud hosting, image storage, push notifications, analytics, crash reporting and payment services. Do not leave out SDKs or providers integrated into the App.

4. Permissions, location and notifications

The App may request device permissions only when needed for a feature. If the App requests access to location, camera, photos, files, contacts or notifications, explain here exactly what is accessed, why it is needed, whether access is optional, and whether the information is transmitted or stored. If the App does not request a permission, remove references to it from this section.

5. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain accounts and records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and why it is needed. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Account deletion and data requests

You can currently request account deletion from within the DIBZ App using its account deletion option. Follow the in-app instructions to submit a request. We will process valid requests and delete or de-identify associated personal data unless we must retain particular information for legal, security, fraud-prevention or legitimate record-keeping reasons. Any retained information will be restricted to the applicable purpose and retained only as required.

You can also request deletion without accessing the App by visiting our DIBZ account deletion page and following the instructions there. We will process valid requests and delete associated personal data unless a specific retention exception applies; any retained data will be limited to the purpose and period required.

For privacy questions or to ask about a data request, email dibz.co.in@gmail.com. Include enough information for us to locate your account, but do not send your password or verification codes.

7. Children's privacy

DIBZ is not intended for children under the age at which they can independently consent to data processing under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us at dibz.co.in@gmail.com so we can review the request.

8. Your choices

You may choose not to provide optional information, manage device permissions through your device settings, and stop using the App. Some features may not work if information or permissions needed for those features are not provided. You may contact us about access, correction or deletion requests, subject to applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our data practices or legal requirements change. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the effective date above. Please review this page periodically.
